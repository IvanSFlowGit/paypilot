# Privacy notice template

This is a client-facing template.
The client (the controller) adapts it and publishes it under its own name.
Replace every `[BRACKETED]` field.
This template is drafting support, not legal advice; the client should have it reviewed before publishing.

Bracketed placeholders to complete:

- `[CLIENT LEGAL NAME]` - the controller's registered name.
- `[CLIENT CONTACT EMAIL]` - the controller's data-protection contact.
- `[RETENTION MONTHS]` - the retention window, default 12 months.
- `[JURISDICTION]` - the region where processing occurs, e.g. UK/EU (London).

---

## Privacy notice - failed-payment recovery

**Controller:** [CLIENT LEGAL NAME]
**Contact:** [CLIENT CONTACT EMAIL]

This notice explains how [CLIENT LEGAL NAME] processes your personal data when a subscription payment fails and we contact you to help you update your billing.

### What we process

- Your name and email address, used only to send you a message about the failed payment.
- Billing metadata about the failed invoice: the invoice reference, the amount, the currency, the reason the payment failed, and whether it was later recovered.
- Identifiers that link the invoice to your account with us and with our payment processor.

We do not collect or store your card details.
Payment is only ever taken on a secure page hosted by our payment processor, Stripe.

### Why we process it, and our lawful basis

- We process your data to recover a payment that failed on your existing subscription with us.
- Our lawful basis is our **legitimate interest** in recovering revenue owed to us, and the **performance of our contract** with you.
- We do not rely on consent for this, because we are acting on the subscription you already have with us.

### Who processes it on our behalf

- We use PayPilot, operated by Streamflow Solutions, as a **processor** to draft and send the recovery message and to record whether the invoice was recovered.
- The processor acts only on our instructions under a data processing agreement.
- We use Stripe as our payment processor and Resend to deliver email.

### How long we keep it

- Recovery records are kept for [RETENTION MONTHS] months after the invoice is closed, then deleted.
- Your name and email are not stored in the recovery ledger; they are used only to send the message and are not retained there.

### Where it is processed

- Processing takes place in [JURISDICTION].

### Your rights

You have the right to:

- ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you (access and portability),
- ask us to delete your data (erasure),
- object to this processing,
- ask us to correct inaccurate data.

To exercise any of these, contact us at [CLIENT CONTACT EMAIL].
We will respond within the statutory time limit.
You also have the right to complain to your data-protection supervisory authority.
