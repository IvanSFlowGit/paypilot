Update your payment method

This is a demonstration page for the PayPilot portfolio project. In production, this route is the hosted card-update flow (for example, Stripe's customer portal), where a customer would securely re-enter their card details after a failed payment.

No real card data is collected here. PayPilot's dunning agent links to this single, allow-listed URL so recovery emails have exactly one safe call to action.
